How one question is handled

  1. You askBy voice or by typing. Say “Hey Sarathi” and it starts listening.
  2. It works within its limitsIt uses only the tools it has been given, and shows its progress as it goes.
  3. It answers twiceA short answer spoken aloud, and the full table or draft on screen.
  4. It asks when unsureAn agent that is not confident asks you, and your answer is reused from then on.

What it may do

  • Read mail and prepare drafts
  • Query your reporting databases, read only
  • List the agents and message them
  • Read files

What it may not do

  • Send or delete mail
  • Write or edit files
  • Run commands on the machine
  • Reach the open internet or start new agents

Why you can trust it

These limits sit in its configuration, not in its instructions. It runs on one machine inside your network, and database questions are read only and rolled back afterwards.

Questions people ask

The short answers. Anything else, ask the team directly.

Do you need access to our live systems?

Only if you want it. You can start the free month with dummy data and move to your real data and access whenever you are comfortable. Either way we read from your systems with read-only access and write only to what we build.

Will the AI send anything on its own?

No. Agents draft the reply, the report or the posting, and a person on your team reads and approves it before anything leaves.

Which systems can you connect to?

Anything with a database, an export, an API or a mailbox. That covers ERPs such as SAP, Oracle and NetSuite, SQL databases, Microsoft 365 and SharePoint, spreadsheets, bank files and web portals.

What happens after the free month?

You keep only what is useful, and the charges are kept small. A one-time setup is a single fee; work that runs every day or week is a low monthly fee. Nothing carries on unless you ask.

Who owns what you build?

You do: the running system, the source code and a handover your team can maintain.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Both. Most of the work is done remotely, and we come on site when the job needs it.
